# Handouts

Supplementary reference material that supports the weekly lectures but isn't tied to any single week — math background, tooling references, and a curated reading list. Use these as background reading or as a quick reference while working through the [`labs/`](../labs/) and [`assignments/`](../assignments/).

| Handout | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Linear Algebra and Calculus Primer](handout01-math-primer.md) | The specific math prerequisites this course leans on: vectors, matrices, dot products, the chain rule, gradients |
| [Backpropagation Derivation](handout02-backpropagation-derivation.md) | A from-scratch, fully worked derivation of backpropagation for a small network, going one level deeper than the Week 1 lecture |
| [PyTorch Quickstart](handout03-pytorch-quickstart.md) | The minimal PyTorch you need for the labs and assignments: tensors, autograd, `nn.Module`, training loops |
| [Paper Reading List](handout04-paper-reading-list.md) | The original papers behind each week's major topic, for students who want to go to the primary source |

None of these are required reading in the way the weekly lecture notes are, but students who feel shaky on the math, on PyTorch itself, or who want to read the original research behind a topic should start here.
